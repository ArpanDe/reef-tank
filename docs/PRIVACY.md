# Reef Tank privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026.

Reef Tank is a screensaver for Roku devices. It does not collect, store, share or sell any
personal information.

- **No network access.** Reef Tank makes no internet connections. All of its art is inside the app.
- **No accounts, analytics, tracking or ads.**
- **Settings stay on your Roku.** The choices you make on Reef Tank's settings screen (art style,
  scrolling, fish, clock, lighting and visitors) are saved in your Roku device's storage for this
  app only. They are never sent anywhere, and they're deleted when you remove Reef Tank.

Your Roku device and account are covered by [Roku's own privacy policy](https://www.roku.com/legal/privacy),
which Reef Tank has no part in.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions:
open an issue at [github.com/ArpanDe/reef-tank](https://github.com/ArpanDe/reef-tank/issues).
